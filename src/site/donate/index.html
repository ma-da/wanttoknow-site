Donate to WantToKnow.info

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, we depend on donations from people like you. Your financial contributions greatly assist WantToKnow.info in becoming ever more effective both in spreading the word about major cover-ups and inviting us to work together to create a more free, informed, and caring society. Your tax-deductible donation, however large or small, makes a difference.

All donations go to PEERS (Public Education and Empowerment Resource Service), the nonprofit under which WantToKnow.info operates.

PEERS is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, and donations are tax deductible to the maximum extent permitted by law.

We welcome donations by credit card, check, money order, direct bank transfer, or your bank's free and easy Bill Pay. For credit cards and online giving, use one of the secure options below.

We especially appreciate monthly donations, which provide steady support for our educational work and can be set up through Donorbox, PayPal, or your bank's Bill Pay service.
