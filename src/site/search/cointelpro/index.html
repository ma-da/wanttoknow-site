On March 8, 1971, an activist group calling itself the Citizens' Commission to Investigate the FBI broke into a local FBI office in Media, Pennsylvania, and discovered evidence that the FBI had been conducting illegal domestic surveillance, infiltration, and disruption campaigns against civil rights and anti-war activist groups.
